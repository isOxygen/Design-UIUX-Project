# Validation and evidence

## What is known
- The screenshots show two distinct categories: Status for monitoring, and Manage for setup and service actions.
- The live application was used before the course. Informal user feedback was positive about separating monitoring from setup and management. That makes the choices easier to distinguish when connecting with different protocols or devices. The feedback informed the decision to keep the split.
- A walkthrough confirmed the clickable prototype works. The live-app feedback is qualitative and separate from that walkthrough.
- The course author confirmed that the FigJam cleanup, wireframes and component review are complete.

## Test protocol
**Prompt:** “You already have a subscription and need to set it up on another device. Show where you would get setup details.” For the second task: “Your app accepts a link that stays up to date. Show where you would get one.” Start from Status for each; do not name navigation labels. For a recovery scenario, point the participant to the simulated unavailable state without giving the recovery action.

1. Ask one peer to try the flow and record three actual observations separately from interpretations.
2. Test with at least three relevant participants. Obtain permission for anonymized notes; no account credentials, personal identifiers or live links. Log first tap, completion without hints, seconds, scroll gestures and recovery. For label validation, ask participants to explain the two setup labels before showing their results.
3. Proposed criterion: at least three of three find the assigned entry unaided within 30 seconds. This is a target, not a result. If doing a follow-up, check whether people distinguish monitoring from setup and find the right action. Scrolling is an optional secondary observation.
